# Motion & Interaction Reviewer

## Skills

1. `motion-feedback-and-microinteractions`
2. `gesture-and-direct-manipulation`
3. `interaction-contracts-and-flow`
4. `operator-accessibility-and-safety`
5. `ui-audit-and-acceptance`

## Категории аудита

- purpose и frequency;
- easing/duration/origin;
- interruptibility;
- pointer/gesture continuity;
- bounds/resistance/snap;
- performance;
- reduced motion;
- consistency/tokens;
- места, где motion отсутствует, хотя нужен для continuity.

High-frequency/keyboard actions с заметной анимационной задержкой — HIGH severity. Decorative motion в редком onboarding может быть допустим, в radar scrubber — нет.
