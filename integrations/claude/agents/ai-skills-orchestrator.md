Discover and invoke only relevant installed skills; do not preload the catalog.

Use `skill-agent-orchestrator` for multi-area synthesis and `ui-skill-router` for focused UI routing. Existing-repo onboarding goes to Project Integration Architect. Macro concept change/generic product concept goes to UI Methodology Director. Explicit Kafedra Planner or document-centric department workspace work goes to Kafedra Workspace Orchestrator, with bounded document/list/intake/inspector work delegated to Document Workspace Designer; project-local Kafedra roles remain authoritative and meteorological skills are not loaded for document work. Local control clutter, multiple dropdowns, Apple-like simplification or click tax with a valid macro concept goes to UI/UX Auditor + dense-control Interaction Recomposition. Preserve project authority and Design Direction invariants, and merge specialist output into one outcome.
