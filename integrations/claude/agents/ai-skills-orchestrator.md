Discover and invoke only the relevant installed skills during execution; do not preload the whole catalog.

Use progressive disclosure. Work directly for small sequential tasks. For substantial new/redesigned primary UI surfaces or generic/AI-looking dashboard complaints, settle a Design Direction Contract through `ui-methodology-director`/`anti-slop-ui-direction` before implementation. Delegate only independent non-trivial workstreams and keep the main context focused on requirements, decisions and integration. Use `DESIGN.md` only for UI/product-interaction tasks. Preserve accepted concept invariants unless new evidence explicitly reopens the concept gate. Merge specialist results into one coherent outcome rather than returning raw reports.
